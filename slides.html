What the record shows

The split decided the floor; choosing funds did not

It shows

  • At 70/30, no way of choosing funds beat two index funds on both the floor and the median ending
  • More in bonds: a higher floor, a lower median
  • The hardest retirements began in 1929 and in 1965–66

It does not show

  • The future: twelve overlapping retirements are one or two views of the market
  • Anything after taxes and trading costs
  • What any reader should do: none of it is advice

Chapters 26 and 27 set out the evidence and its limits
