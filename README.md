# The Retiree's Portfolio

*Active Selection versus Indexing over Thirty Years* -- by Mudit Wahal.

A book-length backtest of a retiree who puts $1 million into Vanguard funds and draws a rising income for twenty
years or more: holding cash or bonds alone, holding the S&P 500, a plain two-fund index mix, and three ways of
choosing the best-ranked funds each January -- from every start year since 1995, not one lucky one. Prices through 2026-10-09.

`index.html` is the book: one self-contained page with every table and chart; it works offline (only the fonts
are fetched from the web).
